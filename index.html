Hi, I am Nafeesath K A

I am a Junior Research Fellow (JRF) at the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences, Indian Institute of Technology Tirupati, and a UGC-NET awardee in Population Studies with All India Rank 2.

My research examines the social effects of migration beyond remittances; its implications for labour force participation, autonomy, housing security, and elderly wellbeing among populations in the Global South. I am trained in quantitative demographic and spatial methods as well as qualitative frameworks, and I integrate these approaches to study developing contexts. I hold an M.Sc. in Biostatistics and Demography from the International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS), Mumbai, where I was awarded a Government of India Fellowship.

My work has been presented at national and international conferences including the 5th Annual International Migration Conference (IIMAD & ICWA) and the National Conference at Madras School of Social Work.

I am actively seeking opportunities in demography, population studies, migration, and related fields.
