Current Research

Migration &
Gender
Female Labour Force Participation and Male Out-Migration in Kerala
Investigating the autonomy paradox in Kerala: despite high educational attainment, women in households with migrant husbands show persistently lower labour force participation. This project models the structural and household-level determinants of this pattern using NFHS, NSSO, and Kerala Migration Survey data, with attention to caste, religion, and district-level heterogeneity.

Geospatial Mapping of Demographic and Health Inequalities
Applying spatial analysis and geospatial mapping techniques to identify geographic clusters of vulnerability in population outcomes — including mortality, fertility differentials, and migration patterns. Integrates GIS with demographic survey data to produce district- and state-level visual analyses for evidence-based policy targeting.

Machine Learning Applications in Demographic Research
An emerging line of inquiry applying classification, clustering, and predictive modelling to demographic and health survey data.
